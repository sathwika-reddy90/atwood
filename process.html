03 / Process

From first conversation to handover

Our design process captures exactly what you want; our execution process makes sure it is built that way. Here is what happens at each of the ten stages.

The ten stages

A structured process ensures every project is handled with precision: consultation, planning and concept development, then sourcing, installation and a final quality review.

Design process

  1. 01
    Concept designA detailed conversation to learn your needs, agree the scope and understand how the space should look and work.
  2. 02
    Schematic designSpatial planning and 3D visual concepts that set the overall design direction.
  3. 03
    Detailed designThe design evolves with you, detail by detail, and we choose the materials together.
  4. 04
    Construction drawingsComplete working drawings for every trade: electrical layouts, false ceilings, carpentry and more.
  5. 05
    Site supervisionOur designers visit the site regularly to make sure the design is built as drawn.

Execution process

  1. 06
    Quantity surveyingA detailed cost estimate based on the chosen design and materials.
  2. 07
    ProcurementOur procurement team selects and buys furnishings, fixtures and accessories, tracks orders and checks every delivery.
  3. 08
    Project planningA detailed timeline setting out the order of the work and how long each stage takes.
  4. 09
    Site managementA dedicated site supervisor runs the day-to-day work, checks materials, organises the crew and resolves problems.
  5. 10
    Quality controlAn internal review confirms the work meets industry standards before handover.

Why one team matters

When the people who design your space also manage its construction, nothing gets lost between the drawing and the site.

Our designers visit the site while work is in progress, and a committed site supervisor manages day-to-day activities, monitors materials and resolves problems as they arise.
