Ten stages, one team

Five stages of design, five of building. The people who draw your space are the people who build it.

Stages 1 to 4 of 10

  1. Design

    First, we listen

    A detailed conversation to learn your needs, agree the scope and understand how the space should look and work.

  2. Design

    Then we plan the space

    Spatial planning and 3D visual concepts that set the overall design direction.

  3. Design

    We refine every detail

    The design evolves with you, detail by detail, and we choose the materials together.

  4. Design

    We draw it for every trade

    Complete working drawings: electrical layouts, false ceilings, carpentry and more.

  5. Design

    We visit the site

    Our designers check the work regularly so it is built exactly to plan.

  6. Build

    We price it exactly

    A detailed cost estimate based on the chosen design and materials, before work starts.

  7. Build

    We buy and check materials

    Our procurement team sources furnishings, fixtures and fittings and checks every delivery.

  8. Build

    We schedule the work

    A project timeline that sets the order of every task and how long each one takes.

  9. Build

    A supervisor runs the site

    Day-to-day management of materials, the crew and any problem that comes up.

  10. Build

    Last, we check everything

    An internal quality review against industry standards before we hand over the keys.

How does the process work?

We start with a consultation to understand your requirements. We then develop design concepts for your approval, and once the design is final we manage the execution through to handover.

Can you work with my budget?

Yes. We plan the space and choose materials to give you a beautiful, functional result within the budget you set.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the size and complexity. We aim to deliver within 30 days, though some projects need longer.

Can I bring my own ideas?

Of course. We work with you at every stage so the space reflects your personality and how you live.

Which styles do you design in?

Modern, traditional, minimalist, industrial, luxury, Scandinavian and contemporary, each tailored to your taste.

How do I get started?

Book a free consultation online or call us. We'll walk you through the first steps and answer your questions.
