Ten stages, one team

Five stages of design and five of execution. The people who draw your space are the people who build it.

Stages 1 to 4 of 10

  1. 01Design

    Concept design

    A detailed conversation to learn your needs, agree the scope and understand how the space should look and work.

  2. 02Design

    Schematic design

    Spatial planning and 3D visual concepts that set the overall design direction.

  3. 03Design

    Detailed design

    The design evolves with you, detail by detail, and we choose the materials together.

  4. 04Design

    Construction drawings

    Complete working drawings for every trade: electrical layouts, false ceilings, carpentry and more.

  5. 05Design

    Site supervision

    Our designers visit the site regularly to make sure the design is built as drawn.

  6. 06Build

    Quantity surveying

    A detailed cost estimate based on the chosen design and materials.

  7. 07Build

    Procurement

    Our procurement team selects and buys furnishings, fixtures and accessories, tracks orders and checks every delivery.

  8. 08Build

    Project planning

    A detailed timeline setting out the order of the work and how long each stage takes.

  9. 09Build

    Site management

    A dedicated site supervisor runs the day-to-day work, checks materials, organises the crew and resolves problems.

  10. 10Build

    Quality control

    An internal review confirms the work meets industry standards before handover.
