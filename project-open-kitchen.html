Overview

An L-shaped kitchen open to the hall, with carved doors and an arched niche giving the space its character. A cove ceiling with warm light ties the kitchen and the hall together.

In the room

  • Base cabinetsAn L-shaped run in deep blue, under a black countertop.
  • Wall cabinetsBlack-framed glass shutters above a marble-effect backsplash.
  • DoorsDark doors with carved floral motifs and slim horizontal inlays.
  • Arched nicheA soft arch cut into the wall beside the doors.
  • PanellingA fluted white wall panel edged in brass at the entrance to the hall.
  • CeilingA cove ceiling with a warm strip light, downlights and a ceiling fan.
