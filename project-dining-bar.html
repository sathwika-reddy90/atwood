Overview

Overview

A dining space that opens onto a bar counter, framed by lit display cabinets and a wall of teal drapes. The ceiling, the joinery and the lighting are designed together, so the room reads as one composition from the table and from the bar.

In the room

  • Display unitsTall cabinets with glass shelves, black frames and warm vertical lighting.
  • DrapesFloor-to-ceiling teal curtains that soften the whole wall.
  • Dining setA white-topped table on a gold base, with upholstered chairs on gold-tipped black legs.
  • Bar counterA counter faced in black marble-effect stone, with high stools.
  • PendantsOpen hexagonal pendant lights over the bar, with plants hung above them.
  • CeilingA dropped ceiling with a reflective inset panel, a chandelier and linear lighting.
