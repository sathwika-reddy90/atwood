Overview

Overview

A child's bedroom organised around one idea: the night sky. Illustrated glass doors, a scalloped headboard and a lit mirror turn storage and furniture into the theme, while the study corner keeps it a room to grow up in.

In the room

  • Sliding wardrobeThree full-height doors in sky-blue glass, printed with astronauts and set in slim black frames.
  • HeadboardDeep-blue upholstered panels with rounded tops, fixed to the wall like a row of standing stones.
  • Storage bedA white bed base with storage, matched to the low drawers beneath the wardrobe.
  • Lit mirrorA full-length mirror with a playful cut-out outline and a warm glow behind its edge.
  • Study cornerA desk with a white drawer unit and a world map covering the wall above it.
  • LightingRecessed downlights with a warm strip of light along the wardrobe wall.
